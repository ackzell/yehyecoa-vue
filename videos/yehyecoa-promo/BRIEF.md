## Intent

Promote (not tour) yehyecoa-vue: a focused Vue SFC playground built for teaching.
All three angles merged into one arc: the name as the hook (yehyecoa, Nahuatl: "to try;
to experiment; to rehearse"), a playground minus the noise, rehearsing together (live
workshop sessions + broadcast mode), and a safety net (snapshots, diffs, restore). Closes on
its home inside amoxtli-vue lessons. No tagline — the word's meaning carries it.
